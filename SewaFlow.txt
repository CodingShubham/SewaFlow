--Why we need BusinessCongig Schema

=>It is the per-user WhatsApp connection record stored in MongoDB instead of in a file.
Every business owner who connects their WhatsApp gets one BusinessConfig document in the database — containing their specific credentials, scoped to their userId.

We refactored the architecture to make it more scalable. Instead of embedding WhatsApp-specific fields inside BusinessConfig, we separated them into a dedicated Integration model. Now BusinessConfig stores only business information, while all external services (WhatsApp today, TikTok, Google Sheets, Shopify, etc. tomorrow) are managed through the generic Integration model. This avoids changing the business schema whenever a new integration is added.
